Before you start

  1. Open Olympus/OlyVia in another tab and log in first.
  2. Keep at least one Olympus tab open while practicing.
  3. If a slide does not open, allow pop-ups/new tabs for this file/browser.

How to study

  • Select a category like Skin, Kidney, GIT, Lung, or Only neoplasms.
  • Open a slide, describe it first, then check the diagnosis inside Olympus.
  • Score yourself here: “Didn’t know” returns in 2 min; “Knew it” returns in 4 hours.
